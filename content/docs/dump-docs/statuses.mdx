## Payment Status

| Status | Meaning | Hospital action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pending` | Payment initiated, not yet confirmed | Keep invoice open |
| `succeeded` | Payment confirmed | Mark invoice paid |
| `failed` | Payment failed | Keep invoice open, show retry option |
| `expired` | Not completed within allowed window | Close payment attempt, allow new attempt |
| `reversed` | Payment reversed/refunded | Reopen invoice or record reversal |

## Event Types

- `payment.succeeded`
- `payment.failed`
- `payment.expired`
- `payment.reversed`
